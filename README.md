## ♟️ THE ROI OF HIRING ME

**Clients and hiring managers usually don't care what programming languages I know. They care about bottlenecks, manual labor costs, and blind decision-making.** 

I am a dual-degree student in **Business Administration** and **Computer Science**. That means I don't build software in a vacuum. Before I write a single line of Python or configure a database, I ask: *How does this save time, uncover a market gap, or generate revenue?*

Here is what I bring to the table:
*   **The "Brain" (Applied AI):** You have thousands of PDFs, complaints, or reports. I build RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems so you can literally "chat" with your private data and get instant, accurate answers.
*   **The "Hands" (Process Automation):** I write the backend scripts and API webhooks that connect your siloed software, ensuring data flows automatically so your team stops doing manual data entry.
*   **The "Eyes" (Market Analytics):** I scrape unstructured noise from the web (reviews, competitor data) and use NLP to turn it into structured, strategic reports for leadership.
